This report adapts templates and reporting procedures from Microsoft Azure's
Well-Architected Reliability Assessment (WARA).
https://github.com/Azure/Well-Architected-Reliability-Assessment
Inspected revision: 86832273f857b7298b625b26d18e2e0dbb06714c
Upstream assets: src/modules/wara/reports/3_wara_reports_generator.ps1,
Mandatory - Executive Summary presentation - Template.pptx,
Assessment-Findings-Report-v1.xlsx.

The cross-platform generator adds prioritized findings, an advisory environment
overlay and a Word report. Native charts/static summaries replace Excel COM pivots.
It is not Start-WARAReport output, pixel-identical reproduction, or validated
desired state. Keep this notice with redistributed reports.
